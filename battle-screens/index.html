Battle screens

Three build puzzles for the fight. Play each through 5 waves and the Rat King, then pick one.

A. Staff Workshop Gun Hero grid
Buy parts and place them on cells touching the staff. Drop a part on an identical one to merge it into the next tier.
B. Potion Shelf colour sort
Pour coloured layers between flasks. Four layers of one colour cork into a potion that powers the frog's spell.
C. Grimoire Page shape packing
Drop a spell over an identical one to merge it, or partly over a different one to fuse them into a new shape. Ingredients beside a spell power it up. Fights take turns: your spells fire in reading order, then the rats move.

The fights play themselves, turn by turn: every choice is made in the puzzle between waves. Back to Magic Frogs
