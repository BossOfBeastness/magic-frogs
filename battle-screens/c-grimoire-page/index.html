Drag or tap a piece to pick it up, tap again to rotate it.

Drop it on the page: green fits, red does not. Drop on a twin to merge it.

Drop a spell on a different spell to fuse them into one stronger piece. Drop an ingredient on a spell to feed it in, up to two.

Spells cast down the page in order: top row first, left to right. Line up ice before blast, poison before fire, and more, for a combo.
