Drag or tap a piece to pick it up, tap again to rotate it.

Drop it on the page: green fits, red does not. Fully overlap a twin (gold) to merge it.

Overlap a different spell of the same tier to fuse them: the new piece is the shape of both combined. Place an ingredient beside a spell (not on it) to buff every spell it touches.

The fight is turn based: your spells fire in reading order (top row first, left to right), then the rats step. Line up ice before blast, poison before fire, and more, for a combo.
